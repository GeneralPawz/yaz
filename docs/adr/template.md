# NNNN — Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Context

What forces are at play? What makes this decision necessary, and what makes it
hard? State the constraints honestly, including the ones we dislike.

## Decision

What we are going to do, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are now committed to.
Include the costs — an ADR that lists only benefits is a sales pitch, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative, and the specific reason it lost. "It was worse" is not a reason.
